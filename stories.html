ORAL HISTORY

Ask somebody what they remember.

With permission, record an interview with a relative, neighbor, teacher, organizer, artist, or elder.

Ask: What do you remember? What changed? What do people misunderstand? What do you wish somebody had written down?
MICRO-STORY

500 words.

Write a small story about a place, object, song, person, or moment that says something bigger.

Pick one ordinary object. Describe it until the reader understands why it matters.
MEDIA LITERACY

Whose version?

Compare two accounts of the same event. Note what each includes, leaves out, emphasizes, and assumes.

Write down the differences before deciding why the accounts differ.
