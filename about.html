why does this exist?

A PLACE TO CARE.

BLACKOUT is a personal cultural project by Von: a messy intersection of music, art, history, politics, philosophy, literature, and the people who made the world harder to ignore.

The visual language pulls from graffiti, zines, album art, newspaper clippings, hand lettering, murals, and the feeling of finding an incredible song at exactly the moment you needed it.

It is intentionally open. BLACKOUT centers Black and other POC histories and voices while making space for LGBTQ+ people and other marginalized communities.
