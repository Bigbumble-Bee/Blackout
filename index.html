01 / THE IDEA

THIS ISN'T
A MUSEUM.

It's a wall.

BLACKOUT is part zine, part listening room, part history notebook, part street-corner conversation.

It is interested in the people and movements that changed culture, the systems that shape people's lives, and the art people make when somebody tells them to shut up.

It is political because people are political. But it isn't here to perform outrage. It is here to remember, question, make, learn, and give a damn.
