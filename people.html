01 / THE PEOPLE

THE PEOPLE

the folks who changed the conversation.

J.B.
WRITER / THINKER

James Baldwin

American writer whose essays and novels examined race, sexuality, religion, identity, and the moral contradictions of American life.

Start with: The Fire Next Time

A.L.
POET / ORGANIZER

Audre Lorde

Poet and essayist whose work connected race, gender, sexuality, class, anger, survival, and collective action.

Start with: Sister Outsider

T.M.
NOVELIST / EDITOR

Toni Morrison

Novelist whose fiction explored memory, Black identity, history, community, language, and the afterlives of slavery.

Start with: Beloved

M.X.
ORATOR / ORGANIZER

Malcolm X

A major Black political thinker whose ideas developed over time around self-determination, racial justice, internationalism, and human rights.

F.L.H.
ORGANIZER / VOTING RIGHTS

Fannie Lou Hamer

Mississippi voting-rights organizer who challenged racial disenfranchisement and helped bring national attention to Black political exclusion.

C.C.
LABOR / ORGANIZER

César Chávez

Labor organizer associated with farmworker organizing and the movement for better working conditions and collective bargaining.

D.H.
ORGANIZER / ACTIVIST

Dolores Huerta

Labor and civil-rights organizer whose work included farmworker organizing, women's rights, and political participation.

V.D.
WRITER / INDIGENOUS RIGHTS

Vine Deloria Jr.

Indigenous scholar and writer whose work challenged misconceptions about Native peoples and argued for sovereignty and self-determination.

K.DOT
MUSIC / STORYTELLING

Kendrick Lamar

A contemporary artist whose work frequently explores Black identity, community, violence, faith, inequality, fame, and personal conflict.

BM
MUSIC / REGGAE

Bob Marley

Jamaican reggae artist whose music became globally associated with peace, resistance, spirituality, African identity, and liberation.

N.S.
MUSIC / PROTEST

Nina Simone

Musician whose work crossed jazz, classical, blues, and soul while engaging directly with Black freedom and political struggle.

F.F.
PHILOSOPHY / COLONIALISM

Frantz Fanon

Martinican psychiatrist and writer whose work analyzed colonialism, racial identity, violence, and decolonization.
