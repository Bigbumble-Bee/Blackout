A STARTING MAP

HISTORY IS NOT
ONE HALLWAY.

BLACKOUT focuses on histories of Black, Latino, Indigenous, Asian, immigrant, queer, and other marginalized communities while connecting them to larger systems.

BLACK HISTORY

Reconstruction → Civil Rights

Study emancipation, Reconstruction, Jim Crow, the Great Migration, organizing, the Civil Rights Movement, Black Power, and their legacies.

LATINO HISTORY

Migration + Labor + Power

Explore Mexican-American history, farmworker organizing, Puerto Rican and Caribbean histories, immigration policy, and Latino political movements.

INDIGENOUS HISTORY

Sovereignty + Survival

Study Indigenous nations, colonization, removal, boarding schools, sovereignty, language revitalization, land rights, and contemporary organizing.

ASIAN AMERICAN HISTORY

Migration + Diaspora

Explore exclusion laws, labor, internment, immigration, diaspora, community organizing, and Asian American movements.

QUEER HISTORY

Visibility + Resistance

Study LGBTQ+ organizing, Stonewall, HIV/AIDS activism, queer art, chosen family, and continuing struggles over equal rights.

LOCAL HISTORY

Start where you live.

County archives, oral histories, newspapers, cemeteries, libraries, murals, and old photographs can reveal stories textbooks miss.
