politics is what happens when somebody has the power to decide your life.

BLACKOUT looks at politics where it intersects with race, ethnicity, immigration, gender, sexuality, disability, class, policing, incarceration, voting, housing, education, labor, and civil and human rights.

The point isn't to tell you what political decision to make. The point is to give you enough context to make your own decision.

CIVIL RIGHTS

Law on paper vs. life

Compare constitutional rights, civil-rights statutes, court decisions, enforcement, and lived experiences.

Look at the actual law or court decision before relying on somebody else's summary.
CRIMINAL JUSTICE

Who gets policed?

Study policing, prosecution, sentencing, incarceration, bail, juvenile justice, and disparities using actual data.

When you see a statistic, check its date, population, methodology, and source.
IMMIGRATION

Who gets to belong?

Learn the legal categories, historical policies, border enforcement, asylum, citizenship, and human stories around migration.

Separate legal categories from political slogans and examine the underlying rules.
LIBERTY

Whose freedom?

Connect freedom of speech, religion, assembly, privacy, voting, and equal protection to political philosophy.

MEDIA

Who controls the story?

Analyze framing, ownership, algorithms, representation, propaganda, political advertising, reporting, and commentary.

CURRENT EVENTS

Slow down before sharing.

Check dates, primary documents, multiple reputable sources, and what population a statistic describes.
